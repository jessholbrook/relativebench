## Findings and boundaries

The directed-transition framing is useful. It should complement standard evaluations,
not imply that all other benchmarks ignore preferences or comparisons. Pairwise human
evaluation already exists; the proposed contribution is incumbent cohorts, frozen
workflows, and an explicit transition protocol. The guide makes that distinction.

The bootstrap used `.get(id, 1)` even when resampling counts were supplied. Clusters
omitted by a draw therefore remained in the sample. The constant-rating bootstrap
test could not detect this. Sparse draws can also remove a scenario's evaluators and
accidentally change category weights. Fixes require new regression tests, not just
more replicates. Negative within-cluster weights can normalize to positive weights;
validate raw records first.

The report displays only three preference bins although the metric uses five ordinal
levels. Readers cannot reconstruct +11 from 41/47/12 alone. Conditional flip rates
also lack denominator explanations. Invented ledger statuses such as “Validated”
are not evidence that this example is reproducible.

## Follow-up gates

The subsequent [participant-free pass](preparticipant-checklist.md) implements
simulations and engineering checks from this list. Some gates require owner or
independent-review decisions, not recruited participants. See that newer checklist
for current status; this document records the original review and PR stack.

- Independent domain review of task/rubric validity and blinding.
- Consent, recruitment, and genuine incumbent exposure verification.
- Simulation-based interval coverage and power/width planning for the actual sparse
  assignment design; a passing deterministic test is not coverage validation.
- Preregistered missingness/exclusion rules, balanced assignment checks, critical-task
  guardrails, and full-precision primary execution before human collection.
- Calibrate the provisional ±5 importance threshold with anchor judgments before
  making non-pilot upgrade/sidegrade claims. Do not tune it on favorable outcomes.
- Report category and subgroup results as exploratory; hold out future tasks to
  audit optimization against the metric. Preference does not substitute for safety.

  These remain aligned with `pilot-plan.md`; no frozen protocol or collection target
is silently changed by this PR stack. No automated fixtures become human evidence.
