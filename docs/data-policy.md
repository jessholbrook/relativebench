# Data and Privacy Policy

Status: **draft for pilot review**

## Data minimization

RelativeBench stores task templates rather than private user conversations by default. User-contributed tasks must be intentionally submitted, screened for secrets and personal information, and transformed into a reusable scenario before evaluation.

## Data classes

- **Public:** redistributable scenarios, model metadata, aggregate results, and consented example outputs.
- **Restricted research:** pseudonymous judgments and licensed-but-nonredistributable task material.
- **Sensitive:** contact details, compensation records, screening responses, and raw submissions awaiting review.

Sensitive data is never included in public snapshots or model prompts without explicit consent and review.

## Identifiers

Public result artifacts use random evaluator identifiers that cannot be joined to account or payment records. Operational identity data is stored separately with more restrictive access.

## Retention

The pilot must specify retention periods before recruitment. Raw sensitive submissions should be deleted after review unless the contributor explicitly consents to research retention. Aggregate and de-identified reproducibility artifacts may be retained indefinitely.

## Model outputs

Each provider's current terms and applicable dataset licenses must be reviewed before outputs are retained or redistributed. When redistribution is not permitted, RelativeBench publishes hashes, derived scores, and sufficient execution metadata without the protected text.

## Participant rights

Recruitment materials describe the study purpose, compensation, expected time, use of judgments, withdrawal window, and contact path. Withdrawal removes linkable unpublished data where feasible; immutable published aggregates are handled according to the consent notice.

## Security incidents

Collection stops when exposed secrets, unexpected personal data, or unauthorized access is detected. Affected artifacts are quarantined, access logs preserved, and publication deferred until review is complete.
